My childhood

Hobbies

Music

I used to sit on the floor next to the radio, waiting for my favorite song to play, pretending I was the one performing it for a huge crowd.

Soccer

After school, I would rush outside with a worn-out ball and play until it got dark, counting imaginary goals in a final match.

Toys

Action figures

I gave my action figures serious missions, building whole storylines where they had to save the world before bedtime.

Building blocks

I stacked blocks into tall towers and called them cities, carefully rebuilding them every time they collapsed.
