My history with chess

I was introduced to chess at a young age, first learning the basic moves out of curiosity and slowly realizing how deep the game actually was. What started as casual games turned into a habit of replaying positions in my head long after the board was packed away. time, chess became less about winning and more about thinking clearly under pressure.

My chess strategies

My approach to chess is simple and practical. I focus on controlling the center early, developing pieces quickly, and avoiding unnecessary risks. I prefer solid positions over flashy sacrifices, slowly building pressure until my opponent makes a mistake. Patience and consistency matter more to me than clever tricks.

What platform I use to play

Most of my games are played online using Chess.com, mainly because it’s easy to access and offers strong opponents at any time. I like reviewing games afterward to understand where I went wrong and how I can improve. The platform keeps me disciplined and exposed to different play styles.

What days I play

I usually play chess on quiet days when my mind is clear, mostly during the week in the evenings. These sessions help me slow down and think strategically after a long day. On weekends, I play fewer games but spend more time analyzing past ones.
